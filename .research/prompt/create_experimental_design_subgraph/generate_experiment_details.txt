# Experiment Strategy
────────────────────────────────────────────────
Experiment 1 – Head-to-Head Alignment Benchmark
Purpose: Show that SC-PSPO is a true drop-in replacement that beats tuned baselines while using zero manual β tuning.
Design
1. Models:  Llama-2-7B-chat (primary) and Zephyr-7B-Gemma-SFT (replication).
2. Data:  Argilla-DPO-Mix-7 K (binary) + Reddit TL;DR (soft counts) concatenated.
3. Methods:  SC-PSPO, DPO, DiscoPOP, GDPO, BPPO.  Each gets exactly one run with the hyper-parameters given in the proposal; baselines use the authors’ recommended β or default grid-search file where required.
4. Hardware: one A100-80 GB per run, gradient accum. so that effective batch = 64.
5. Metrics
   • Quality: MT-Bench and AlpacaEval win-rate versus GPT-4.
   • Calibration: ECE and −log likelihood on a 10 k held-out preference set.
   • Stability: max grad-norm, % runs diverged (5 seeds).
   • Throughput: tokens / s.
Pass / Fail Criteria
SC-PSPO must (i) match or exceed the best tuned baseline’s win-rate, (ii) cut ECE by ≥50 %, and (iii) show zero divergence without gradient clipping.
Demonstrates: practical superiority in quality, calibration, and stability while eliminating β sweeps.
────────────────────────────────────────────────
Experiment 2 – Robustness to Noisy or Adversarial Votes
Purpose: Validate the uncertainty-weighting mechanism and bounded gradient under extreme label noise.
Design
1. Take 1 k preference pairs from the Human Harmless subset.  For each pair, synthesise extra “votes” so that the empirical y∈{0,0.25,0.5,0.75,1}.  Also generate 20 % adversarial flips (winner↔loser).
2. Train three fresh Llama-2-7B copies on only these 1 k pairs for 500 steps via (a) SC-PSPO, (b) DPO with β tuned, (c) BPPO.
3. Log per-step loss, grad-norm, and accuracy on a clean validation slice (no corruption).
4. Extend corruption level from 0→60 % in a controlled sweep; repeat once per level.
Metrics
   • Convergence rate: steps to reach 80 % clean-set accuracy.
   • Failure rate: runs that diverge or whose grad-norm >10.
   • Final ECE on clean validation.
Demonstrates: SC-PSPO’s epistemic down-weighting and bounded gradient keep training stable and calibrated even when labels are extremely unreliable, whereas baselines become brittle or explode.
────────────────────────────────────────────────
Experiment 3 – Vote-Count Efficiency Study
Purpose: Show that SC-PSPO extracts extra performance from multiple votes per pair, enabling cheaper data collection strategies.
Design
1. Reddit TL;DR has 5 GPT-4 votes per pair.  Construct four training settings:
   S1 – Majority vote (1 bit)  •  S2 – 3 random votes  •  S3 – all 5 votes  •  S4 – 5 votes + an extra synthetic vote so total = 6 (tests diminishing returns).
2. For each setting, subsample the dataset so that the total number of human-labels processed is identical (e.g., fewer pairs when more votes per pair).
3. Train SC-PSPO and DPO (majority only) for two epochs; evaluate on MT-Bench + calibration set.
4. Compute “label-cost-adjusted quality” = win-rate ÷ total votes consumed.
Expected Outcome
   • SC-PSPO’s win-rate rises monotonically S1→S3 and plateaus at S4, giving 15–25 % higher cost-adjusted quality than DPO.
   • DPO shows no benefit from extra votes because it collapses them to a single bit.
Demonstrates: the count-aware soft-label design yields tangible sample-efficiency gains and makes crowdsourcing with repeated votes economically attractive.
────────────────────────────────────────────────
If all three experiments succeed, we have evidence from complementary angles—raw benchmark strength, robustness under noise, and sample efficiency—that SC-PSPO is a superior, temperature-free replacement for current preference-learning losses.
